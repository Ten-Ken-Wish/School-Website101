ABOUT OUR ACADEMY

About Taktsang Academy

Taktsang Academy is a wellbeing school located in Paro, Satsam. We believe in not just academics but also in the holistic development of our students through proper values, cultural knowledge, and various opportunities to spark curiosity and give students a platform to showcase their capabilities.

Learn More →

Our Mission

We nurture curious minds, compassionate hearts, and responsible citizens through meaningful learning, strong values, wellbeing and lifelong growth

Our Vision

"Taktsang Academy will be a place where education empowers the whole child- for life, not just for school"

Our Motto

Be Confident

What Makes Taksang Unique

Wellbeing Focus

We nurture the mind, body, and spirit through a holistic approach that values academic, physical, emotional, social, and spiritual wellbeing.

Vast Opportunities

Students are encouraged to explore their interests and talents through academics, sports, culture, leadership, creativity, and extracurricular activities.

Modernity with Cultural Roots

We embrace modern education and opportunities while remaining grounded in Bhutanese culture, values, traditions, and identity.

Strong Community

We build a supportive community where students, teachers, and families work together, fostering respect, belonging, and shared growth.
