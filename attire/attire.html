THE TAKTSANG ATTIRE

Tradition, identity and purpose.

At Taktsang Academy, attire is more than a uniform. It reflects our Bhutanese identity, encourages a sense of belonging and allows our students and teachers to move comfortably through the different dimensions of school life.

From the Gho and Kira worn during the regular school day to sports, yoga and activity attire, each form of dress has its own place within the Academy.
