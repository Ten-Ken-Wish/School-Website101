OUR STORY

A different way
to grow.

Taktsang Academy was established with a vision of creating an educational environment where learning extends beyond the classroom.

The Academy brings together academic learning, practical skills and personal development, encouraging students to understand not only what they learn, but also who they are becoming.

Rooted in Bhutanese values and guided by a wellbeing-centred approach, Taktsang seeks to create a community where young people can learn with curiosity, live with purpose and grow with confidence.

Established 11 November 2025
